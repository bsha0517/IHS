# 1. PURPOSE

P3.5 is:

# LABORATORY / RADIOLOGY & CLINICAL ORDER HANDOFF WORKFLOW

This is NOT another whole-system audit.

The objective is:

> Make a doctor's Laboratory or Imaging order flow coherently from the Encounter into the appropriate Lab/Radiology operational queue, through processing and result verification/reporting, and safely back into the Encounter and Patient 360.

Conceptually:

Doctor Encounter
→ Clinical Order
→ Lab/Radiology Work Queue
→ Accept/Assign
→ Process
→ Enter Result/Report
→ Verify/Finalize
→ Result available to Doctor
→ Patient 360
→ Encounter context

Do NOT rebuild the ClinicalOrder architecture.
