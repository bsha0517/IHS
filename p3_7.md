# 1. PURPOSE

P3.7 is:

# BILLING / POS / CASHIER WORKFLOW

This is NOT another whole-project audit.

The objective is:

> Make the operational financial workflow coherent from billable clinical activity through Charge review, invoice creation, payment collection, receipt, refund/void handling and cashier visibility while preserving the existing centralized billing and accounting architecture.

Clinical / Operational Activity
→ Charge

→ Invoice
→ Payment
→ Receipt
→ Accounting Posting

Invoice void where valid

Payment refund/reversal where valid
