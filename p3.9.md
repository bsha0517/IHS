# 3. CRITICAL DEFERRED ITEMS TO REVIEW

Explicitly inspect these two issues.

## A. Pharmacy return financial reversal

P3.6/P3.7 confirmed:

DispensingReturn restores physical inventory, but currently does NOT safely reverse the original:

* Charge/revenue
* COGS

The current Refund model is invoice-level/lump-sum and does not have line-level linkage adequate to express a safe pharmacy-item return.

Do NOT create a half-correct shortcut.

P3.9 is the correct phase to decide whether the existing financial architecture can support a narrow, correct solution.

More guidance appears later.

## B. Failed accounting postings caused by closed financial periods

P3.7 confirmed:

An operational action such as Invoice issuance can succeed, while the asynchronous accounting event later fails because the financial period is closed.

The outbox retries/dead-letters the posting and correctly does NOT write into the closed period.

This architectural behavior may remain valid.

But Accountant/Admin need operational visibility and a recovery path.

Do not silently leave financially-unposted operational transactions invisible.
